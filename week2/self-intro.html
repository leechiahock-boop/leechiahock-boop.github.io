"Go all in"

Go in heavy, but always stay calm. Keep trading with discipline and don’t expect to buy at the absolute bottom or sell at the absolute top. No one can predict every market reversal perfectly. Technical analysis is not about being right 100% of the time. It’s about understanding the trend, identifying support and resistance, and managing risk properly. Never obsess over taking profit at the highest point or buying at the lowest point. What truly matters is following your trading plan, controlling your position size, setting your stop-loss, and taking reasonable profits. Trading isn’t about making money the fastest. It’s about staying in the market for the long run. There will always be another opportunity. Patience and discipline are what matter most.
